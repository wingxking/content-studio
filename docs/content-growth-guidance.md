# Platform content growth and editorial restrictions

Checked: 2026-10-10, Asia/Singapore. Original scope: 12 format IDs. Product Hunt and Zhihu were added in the [publishing upgrade](publishing-capabilities-upgrade-2026-10-11.zh-CN.md), making 14 registered platform IDs with separate format/access checks. Format support is not publishing permission or live channel acceptance. Implementation and reusable prompts are described in [prompt workflows](content-prompt-workflows.md); the project skill is [content-editorial](../.agents/skills/content-editorial/SKILL.md).

## What the evidence supports

Improve useful reading/viewing and audience satisfaction through original contribution, specific audience fit, accurate packaging, and a complete answer. Recommendation eligibility, recommendation distribution, monetization eligibility, and paid promotion are separate outcomes. Neither a compliant post nor an AI disclosure guarantees distribution.

**Official guidance** below means an attributed platform statement. **Editorial experiments** are our practical interpretations to test. **Unknown** means no reliable current evidence was obtained. No universal best posting hour, fixed traffic pool, mandatory growth threshold, hashtag count, engagement ratio, or ranking weight is claimed. Published recommendation explanations can be simplified and surface-specific.

## Owner's editorial exclusions

Exclude gender discrimination and sexist framing; religion; war and armed conflict; politics, elections and geopolitical disputes. Additional sensitive themes excluded for this product are hate and harassment, sexual exploitation, graphic violence, exploitation of tragedies, promotion of illegal acts, and individualized medical/legal/financial advice. This is a product scope choice; it does not assert that every platform bans all discussion of these subjects.

Apply exclusions before candidate ranking and throughout outlines, articles, platform text, titles, tags, covers, narration, selected assets, refinements, scheduling, and engagement suggestions. High search volume or engagement potential never overrides them. A neutral explainer, satire, historical account, or indirect hook about the excluded subject is still excluded. Do not automatically replace the user's topic with another topic; return an excluded decision and ask for a permitted brief. Ambiguous subjects receive an uncertain decision and no generated content.

The rule concerns editorial subject matter and discriminatory framing, rather than substring matching. A software guide explaining a content filter may name excluded category labels. Prefer software/AI tools, productivity, design, non-medical science, everyday skills, and culture without religious, political, or war subject matter.

## AI provenance, rights and eligibility

Assess final images/audio/video, not only whether the script was AI-assisted. YouTube requires disclosure for realistic meaningful alterations/generated people, events or scenes and AI music as the main focus; ordinary outline/script/title assistance generally does not need that disclosure. It states the label itself does not limit audience or monetization eligibility. TikTok requires labels for realistic AI image/audio/video and says labeling a compliant video does not itself reduce distribution. A label cannot legalize otherwise prohibited deception. [YouTube AI help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en), [TikTok AI help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content).

Chinese generated/synthetic content labeling measures, effective 2025-09-01, require proactive publisher declaration/use of available labeling functions and prohibit malicious concealment or alteration of labels. Preserve available provenance and use current native platform controls. Platform-specific details still require checking. [Official enacted measures](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm). Reddit's current manipulated-content policy expects transparent AI use and prohibits misleading human-generated presentation; communities may impose stricter rules. [Reddit policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/41180423371156-Manipulated-Content-and-Misleading-Behavior).

Do not invent quotations, statistics, firsthand experience, test results or verification. Link factual sources, distinguish observation date from event/publication date, and retain caveats. Obtain rights for photos, footage, music, voices and likenesses; a public URL or attribution alone is not reuse permission. Include required sponsorship/affiliate disclosures. Do not remove citations or AI labels to pursue reach.

Account login does not establish approved publishing access. Retain the project's [platform permission gates](platform-policies-v2.zh-CN.md). TikTok Direct Post requires user preview/control/consent and audit for public visibility; its intended-use guidance excludes a private upload utility for the developer/team's own accounts. A personal scheduler cannot assume qualification. [Content Sharing Guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines). Reddit automation requires its current App approval; X trend-origin automation remains prohibited. News/professional category eligibility is separate from general account access, particularly for China destinations; do not choose an excluded news subject in the first place.

## Scheduler and learning loop

1. Apply exclusions and evidence checks before ranking. Rank safe topics by audience need, original contribution, source quality, timely relevance, destination fit, and novelty relative to recent work. Keep regional signals separate; do not manufacture a global rank, combine incompatible traffic counts, or fill a quota with weak questions.
2. Keep the user's selected time and IANA timezone. Use observed audience activity where available, a sustainable review capacity and a topic freshness deadline. A suggested time is an experiment awaiting user choice, not a guaranteed platform optimum. YouTube reports no observed long-term viewership benefit from publish-time optimization. [YouTube recommendations guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16533387?hl=en).
3. Check same-account/topic similarity and actual community/category rules. Expired facts, unresolved rights, uncertain AI labels, moderation removals, lost permission or a platform warning place the run on hold. Never compensate by increasing frequency, evading restrictions, deleting/reposting repeatedly or switching accounts.
4. Preserve approval of actual revision, target account, final media and disclosure. An exported prompt or discovery run cannot grant publishing permission. Unknown upload outcomes use reconciliation rather than blind resubmission.
5. For each comparable account/format cohort, choose one editorial variable and one primary metric. Examples: title clarity vs qualified reading, opening demonstration vs retention, reusable checklist vs genuine saves. Avoid mass duplicate public posts as an experiment. Use native title-testing tools where authorized. Record competing explanations; small samples are inconclusive.
6. Use consistent observation windows, for example 24 hours, 72 hours and seven days, adjusted to topic lifecycle. These are suggested comparison windows, not official requirements. Record numerator, denominator, account, format, traffic source, topic age and organic/paid status. Missing analytics are unavailable rather than zero. No views-only diagnosis of suppression.

Record other measured values and windows in notes pending a dedicated analytics design. ## Maintenance and limits

The shared runtime prompt uses a dated guidance version. Update the research and that version together when verified rules change. Recheck material delivery rules and account notices before actual publication; a dated report is not perpetual clearance. No new recurring automation was created by this research.

The generation assessment is model-reported. The code rejects missing, excluded, uncertain or inconsistent structured results, but this does not prove independent semantic moderation. Manual imports/edits and historical drafts still require the skill's editorial review and existing publication preflight. No historical draft or published post was removed or reclassified by this change.
